Common Cyber Threats

Understand online threats and learn how to protect yourself.

Threats You Should Know

🎣

Phishing Attacks

Fraudulent emails or messages trick people into sharing passwords, OTPs or banking details.

Protection: Verify the sender and avoid suspicious links.

💳

Online Payment Scams

Scammers may use fake payment requests or impersonate trusted services to steal money.

Protection: Verify every payment request before approving it.

🪪

Identity Theft

Someone misuses personal information to impersonate another person or commit fraud.

Protection: Keep personal documents and details private.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software can damage devices, spy on activity or steal information.

Protection: Download apps only from trusted sources.

🔓

Password Attacks

Attackers may guess weak passwords or use stolen passwords to access accounts.

Protection: Use unique passwords and two-factor authentication.

📱

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles and messages may be used to trick people into sharing money or information.

Protection: Verify profiles and avoid sharing sensitive information.

How to Stay Safe

What Should You Do If You Face a Cyber Scam?

✓ Stop communicating with the suspected scammer.

✓ Never share your OTP, PIN or password.

✓ Contact your bank immediately if money is involved.

✓ In India, report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 for financial cyber fraud.
